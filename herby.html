How it formats

  • Each product is written as SUPPLIER, NAME, SIZE, TYPE, using the pricelist's name, e.g. CYMRA SUPERBLY ROSEY 10g FLOWER, KIND ROSA CART, AURA 40:20:20 LIVE NIGHT BERRY PASTILLE. Description words like CO2 extraction, biodynamic or organic are left out; INDICA, SATIVA, HYBRID and LIVE RESIN are kept. Cannatrek products always start with CANNATREK.
  • If more than one pricelist product fits a name equally well (like C100 OIL), Herby asks you to pick instead of guessing.
  • If a name is only close to the pricelist spelling (PURPLE RAIN vs PURPLE RAINE), nothing is changed until you tap Yes, use pricelist name or No, keep what I typed. Different products with similar names, like BLUE CHEESE and BLUEBERRY CHEESE, or WALKEN and WALKER, are kept apart.
  • Flowers get their tub size and oils their bottle size from the pricelist, e.g. AVANI BLING BLAU 10g FLOWER, AURA CBD200 30ml OIL. If the size is missing, unclear or different from what you typed, the row is flagged.
  • Medcan codes that come as a flower and a cart (GLU01, CC01, SC01) ask which one. The cart is written with -V, e.g. GLU01-V CART.
  • Scripts marked (dis) on the sheet are labelled Discontinued, and "(dis)" is left out of the message.
  • Shorthand is understood: C200 means CBD200 (like the pricelist's own C100), TB means Tasmanian Botanics, TBC100 is the Tasmanian Botanics CBD100 oil, Full Spectrum means FS, Australian Natural is written as ANTG, and GUMMY or GUMMIES is written as PASTILLE.
  • Names on the inventory's discontinued list are kept exactly as typed and labelled Discontinued. If a name has nothing in common with any pricelist product, it's probably discontinued too and is also kept as typed. Neither blocks copying.
  • Last Repeat becomes 0 Repeats.
  • Scripts expiring within 2 months go under Expiring Soon. If none are, the list has no headings.
  • Products sold as more than one type, like KIND ROSA (flower, oil and cart), are flagged orange and the message can't be copied until you pick one. Only the types the pricelist has for that product are shown. Names that aren't an exact pricelist match are flagged orange or red, and the message can't be copied until you pick one of the pricelist options shown, fix the name, or tap Keep as typed. Expired scripts are left out of the message.
  • Everything stays in your browser. Nothing you paste is saved or sent anywhere.
